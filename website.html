ABOUT THE GAME

Explore the depths of the Lost Exit Realm

Lost Exit Realm is a 2D action-platformer where you explore dark dungeons to find the hidden exit. Choose between the Warrior or Mage to battle enemies using unique abilities like magic, dashing, and projectiles. Collect hidden Keys and Orbs across different levels to unlock the path to victory and complete your mission.

LEVELS

Conquer the different realms of the dungeon.

LEVEL I

This introductory stage requires players to find 2 keys while navigating a basic layout designed to teach core movement and combat against stationary enemies.

LEVEL II

The challenge increases as players must collect 3 keys while facing more strategic enemy placements and tighter platforming sections.

LEVEL III

As the ultimate test, this level demands 4 keys and forces players to survive against the full variety of threats, including the unpredictable Jumping Enemy.

CHARACTERS TO PLAY

MAGE

is an agile, hovering class that utilizes infinite magic orbs and a unique charging system to enter a powerful "Super Saiyan" state. In this mode, she gains double movement speed and a golden aura, allowing her to overwhelm enemies with pure mystical energy.

WARRIOR

is a tactical long-range specialist who uses a high-damage laser rifle with a 15-round ammo capacity. He relies on manual reloading and a quick dash mechanic to dodge attacks and maintain his position on the battlefield.

OBJECTIVES

  • Find the hidden keys and orbs by defeating powerful enemies.
  • Avoid traps and enemy attacks to protect your limited lives.
  • Locate the correct exit door once you have collected all required items.

WHY PLAY?

Ready to dive in? This game offers an intense action-packed combat experience where you can master the unique playstyles of a Warrior or a Mage.

As you explore dangerous levels and defeat hordes of enemies, you’ll need to keep your wits about you to find the correct exit and progress through a dark, immersive world.

With limited lives on the line, every move counts truly testing your accuracy, timing, and skill in a challenge that keeps you right on the edge of your seat.
